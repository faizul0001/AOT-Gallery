Eren Yeager

Attack Titan
Height: 183 cm
Age: 15 (S1-S3) / 19 (Final Season)
Affiliation: Scout Regiment (Special Operations Squad)

Biography & Description

Eren Yeager is the main protagonist of Attack on Titan. He dedicated his life to wiping out Titans after witnessing his mother's tragic death. Later, he discovered his own ability to transform into a Titan, altering the course of humanity's fight for freedom.
